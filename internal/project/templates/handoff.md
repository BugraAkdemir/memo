# handoff.md — session log

Newest entry on top. One entry per session, written at the end of any session
that changed something. It lets the next session (or another model) continue
without reconstructing what happened from the git log.

Each entry:
- **Summary** — what was asked and what was done, for a reader with no memory
  of the conversation.
- **Commit status** — what is safely committed and what is still uncommitted.
- **Verification** — the real commands that were run and their real output;
  say plainly what was NOT verified.
- **Next session** — the single most important next step, by name, plus what
  was deliberately left out of scope and why.
