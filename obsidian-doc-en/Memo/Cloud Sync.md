# ☁️ Cloud Sync

Memo offers Google Drive integration for the secure transfer of your data between devices.

## Privacy and Security: AES-256 E2E
The most critical feature of synchronization is that data **never goes to the cloud in an open state.**
1. **Local Encryption:** Data is encrypted with the AES-256 standard using a **Passphrase** you determine before leaving your device.
2. **End-to-End (E2E):** The encryption key is only with you. Even Google cannot see the content of your data.

## What is Synchronized?
- Semantic Memories
- User Settings
- System Prompts
- Chat Sessions (Optional)

## Setup
1. Go to Settings > Cloud Sync tab.
2. Log in with your Google account.
3. Set a strong passphrase (if you lose this passphrase, you cannot access the data in the cloud).
