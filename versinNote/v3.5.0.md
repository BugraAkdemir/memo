## What's cooking

A few threads already underway that are likely to land here:

- **A dedicated mobile companion app** — a lighter, phone-first way to reach
  your Memo instance, separate from the desktop client.
- **Wider platform reach** — Raspberry Pi / ARM boards and self-hosted
  setups (CasaOS, Docker) are getting first-class support, not an
  afterthought.
- **Memo Swarm** continuing to mature out of Beta — pooling multiple
  machines' compute for local inference.
- **Live Mode** getting refined further after its v3.3.4 debut.

Nothing above is a promise of a ship date or a final feature list — it's a
snapshot of where active work is happening. Check back as v3.5.0 gets
closer.
